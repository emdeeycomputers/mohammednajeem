A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Communication is my foundation
Learning keeps me moving

My professional journey brings together media, digital skills, leadership and entrepreneurship.

I hold a B.Sc in Mass Communication from Ibrahim Badamasi Babangida University, Lapai. My experience includes producing news and publicity content, editorial work, photography, graphic design, data analysis, computer setup & maintenance, internet services and other related computer operations.

During my national service at Bingham University's Directorate of Public Affairs, I supported the university's communication activities through reporting, graphic design, photography and computer operations. Outside the workplace, I have contributed to campus media, volunteer initiatives and community development activities.

Through the NYSC Digital Literacy for All Community Development Group (CDS) in partnership with National Information Technology Development Agency (NITDA), I participated in mass digital literacy advocacy and training of residents in Karu LGA, Nasarawa State. I have also volunteered my creative skills and shared knowledge with others. More recently, BCO Ignite Project Cohort 2 has supported my continued development in data analysis, coding, leadership and career guidance and graphic design.
